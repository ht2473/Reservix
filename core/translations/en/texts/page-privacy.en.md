## General provisions

This policy sets out how employees' personal data are processed in the
talent pool information system. The policy was developed in accordance with
Federal Law No. 152-FZ "On Personal Data".

The data controller is the organization in which the system is deployed.
Processing is carried out for HR purposes: succession planning, assessing how
employees meet position requirements, and organizing their professional
development.

## Data processed

* Account details: surname, first name, patronymic, personnel number, work
  email and phone.
* Employment details: unit, position, hire and transfer dates, tenure,
  education.
* Assessment results: competency proficiency levels, test results, performance
  and potential ratings.
* Talent pool participation and development plan progress.

Special categories of personal data and biometric data are not processed in
the system. Pay information is not transferred to the system.

## Processing principles

Processing is limited to the purposes listed above. The data collected do not
exceed what is needed to achieve them: the system does not store information
that is not used by any procedure.

Access is controlled on two independent grounds: the role defines the actions
allowed, and the visibility scope defines whose data are available. A unit
manager and a branch director have the same role but different data coverage.

## Retention

Account and assessment data are kept for the duration of employment and for
the subsequent period set by the organization's records management rules.
Audit log entries are kept for three years. Generated reports are deleted
ninety days after creation.

## Security measures

* Access control by role and visibility scope, checked on every data access.
* Data transmitted over a secure connection.
* Data operations recorded in an audit log that cannot be modified.
* Document files stored outside the public directory and served only after an
  access check.
* Anonymization on public pages: counts and shares are published, but not data
  about specific employees.

## Data subject rights

Employees have the right to obtain information about the processing of their
personal data, to request correction of inaccurate data, and to appeal the
controller's actions. Requests can be submitted through the feedback form or to
the organization's HR department.
