## Access to the system

Access is granted to the organization's employees through an account created
by the system administrator. Sharing credentials with another person is not
permitted: all actions performed under an account are considered actions of its
owner and are recorded in the audit log.

## User obligations

* Keep confidential the information accessed in the system.
* Do not export employees' personal data outside the organization's network
  without a business need.
* Report any signs of account compromise to the administrator immediately.

## Data accuracy

A user who enters information is responsible for its accuracy. Ratings entered
in breach of the assessment procedure are subject to cancellation by HR with a
stated reason.

## Limitations

The system does not keep HR records and does not replace the payroll system.
Appointment decisions are made by authorized persons of the organization; the
system prepares a justified ranked list but does not make appointments
automatically.
